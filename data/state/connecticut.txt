Public health spotlight
Stay healthy this season: Prevent infections before they start
As flu, COVID-19, and RSV season gets underway, preventing infections is more important than ever. Antibiotics save lives, but they don't work against viruses like the flu, COVID-19, or RSV. Using antibiotics when they aren't needed can contribute to antibiotic resistance, making bacterial infections harder to treat.
This November, the Connecticut Department of Public Health encourages residents to take simple steps to stay healthy:
Stay up to date on recommended flu, COVID-19, and RSV vaccines.
Wash your hands regularly with soap and water.
Keep cuts clean and covered to help prevent infections.
Only take antibiotics when needed and prescribed by a healthcare provider.
Preventing infections helps protect your health, reduces the need for antibiotics, and helps keep these lifesaving medications effective for the future.
